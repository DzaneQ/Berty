# UI Card

Hand cards (UI cards) are an interactive part of the gameplay that the player plays and pays with. The player owning them can see their attributes.

The player can click on the card to select and unselect it.
- Selected card can be put on board to make a board card with it.
- During payment phrase, selected cards are used to be paid with to confirm paid action.

Hand cards are added to the player at the beginning of the turn and removed as they're put on board or paid with.
